Back when I was still in college, in 2022, I remember talking with L about a dream to one day play 100 v 100 capture the flag with in Central Park. There was just something so giddy about sneaking around, blending in with pedestrians and laying low in the grass -- and then exhilarating to launch a coordinated attack with your best homies.

Now I live in SF, and I've always had the same ideas when I walked through Golden Gate Park. A few months after moving in I made a few friends that were also dreaming similar silly ideas.

So we made it happen! [Check out our ruleset here :)](https://goldengatectf.com)
