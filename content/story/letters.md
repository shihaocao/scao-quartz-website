A short film about the end of a relationship viewed from the letters written to each other.

## Making This

This was my first ever production! I had a lot of fun making this, and I got to do all parts of this project: acting, directing, camera, grip, editing, sound, and more!

## Thanks

Thank you to everyone who helped me make this project possible. I am grateful to have the opportunity to create something awesome to capture emotions with friends.
