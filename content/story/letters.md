A short film about the end of a relationship viewed from the letters written to each other.

## Making This

This was my first ever production! I had a lot of fun making this, and I got to do all parts of this project: acting, directing, camera, grip, editing, sound, and more!

## Inspiration on The Train

I spend a lot of time on the Caltrain, and between February and April, I spent time story boarding and shot planning. Two times strangers happened to get involved, both on the evening commute back home.

One time, a voice came on the loud speaker and announced the train would came to a halt in the because there was a vehicle blocking the crossing a few stops ahead. With an unspecified time to get going again, I struck up a conversation with the passenger across from me on the table -- both curius how we would be passing the extra time on the train. They were studying for school, and I told them I was trying to figure out how I wanted to figure out the ending of the script. Together we talked through what things might make sense, and if a happier or sadder ending would be better.

Another time, Stewart and I were jamming out on the train thinking about the upcoming shots we wanted that evening (Annie was coming over to film), and someone across the aisle spoke up and said: "I can't help but listen in, but this sounds like exactly what I just went through -- my ex and I wrote letters to each other in the past -- I think shots highlighting x y or z relate the most..." That was a fun train ride as all three of us were just thinking of what spoke most truly to us, from the girl's side and from the guy's side.

## Thanks

Thank you to everyone who helped me make this project possible. I am grateful to have the opportunity to create something awesome to capture emotions with friends.
