_What if we allocated 3 hours to doom through scroll through our friends' kindred algorithms?_

## Why?

Over the years I've found many short hearwarming or seatclenching short films, and the impressions they have left on me
have punched above their weight class. I have wondered, what are those films for my friends?

And so here's that event! We can binge them all together, and see a glimpse of what tugs at each other's hearts, dreams, and curiosities.

### The Event

Please be prepared to submit exactly one short film, animation, sketch, that is anywhere between 1 to 15 minutes long.

### Critiquing and Scoring

We will form critque groups to discuss, score, and enjoy the films as they are shown. Groups will analyze categories such as: Best Story, Best Acting, Best Cinematography, etc. At the end of the festival, each group will submit
their reviews to a form for final scoring. Participants who submitted the winner of each category will win a small prize!

### Extra Blurbs:

Dm me if you have questions or multiple candidates -- we will also share an extended runner up list (for films that don't make it to submission)
