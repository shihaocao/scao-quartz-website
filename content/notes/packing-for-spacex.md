## Things to Pack for a SpaceX Internship

I originally made this document to help future interns pack. Here are the things I packed for my two summers at SpaceX, and hopefully, you'll find this useful for inspiration. For reference, this is for the Hawthorne office (in LA). I am also a dude. If you are a dudette, change items accordingly.

I was able to fit this all into a backpack, a carry-on, and one 50lb checked bag since I flew in. If you need more space, you're packing too much.

And while I'm here, if you have a car (or access to one), you will have so much more fun in LA.

---

## Clothes for the Office

My favorite outfit to SpaceX was a T-shirt and gray jeans + a light jacket with Stan Smiths. Most people at SpaceX wear this kind of outfit. I gave my final presentation in a SpaceX T-shirt and jeans lol.

A light jacket is the move because sometimes it gets chilly at the office due to AC. Long jeans because it's casual AF, and people don't really wear shorts at the office. You could totally wear sneakers to the office too btw.

Also, if you plan on buying a ton of SpaceX T-shirts, you can pack fewer T-shirts overall and just buy merch as soon as you get there. Merch usually takes ~10 days to get to the office after you order it.

- 10+ T-shirts
- 10+ boxers
- 10+ socks
- 2x pairs of blue/gray jeans (or casual pants)
- 1x running sneakers
- 1x nicer shoes (Stan Smiths)
- 2x hoodies
- 2x light jackets

## Clothes, but for Everything Else

### For Going Out

- 1x Illenium T-shirt (or equivalent haha)

### For Going Out to Nicer Places

- 1x black dress shirt
- 1x black jeans

I don't think I ever actually wore this, but it's good to have if you go out to a nicer place for dinner.

### Office Flair

Many teams wear Hawaiian shirts on Fridays. I never packed this, but if you want to vibe with your team, pick one up. Highly recommended for Boca teams.

- 1x Hawaiian shirt

### Beach

- 1x swim trunks
- 3x casual shorts (Uniqlo/H&M vibes)
- 1x flip flops
- 1x sunglasses (you will want these)

### Outdoors

- 1x hiking shoes/boots, SpaceX-ers love hikes
- 3x hiking socks (thick Costco socks)
- 1x hat/bucket-hat
- 1x hiking pants (really useful for Boca, you can get this reimbursed lol)
- 1x drawstring backpack, really useful for smaller hikes
- 1x North Face Recon backpack (or any backpack with a waist strap), really useful for longer 4+ mile hikes carrying heavier loads
- 1x gym shorts (for gym/active)
- 1x athletic shirt (for gym/active)

### Boca

- 1x steel-toed boots, for Hawthorne teams, SpaceX will buy them for you. For Boca teams, ask your manager ahead of time.
